Accepted architecture meaning

  1. Admitted append-only history is the semantic source; current state is reconstructed rather than independently hand-edited.
  2. One deterministic kernel reduces current meaning and projects actor or subject-context surfaces.
  3. A surface is accumulated current value plus permitted next actions.
  4. A user receives a next value surface; use of that surface may become the next observation.
  5. A gesture creates a typed candidate, not accepted meaning; admission precedes any effect.
  6. Effect execution, provider response, readback, observation, and business outcome remain distinct.
  7. Observations return to append-only history and cause deterministic re-projection.
  8. UI, databases, manifests, receipts, funnels, scores, and provider responses are not independent accepted-meaning authority.

This public page is a projection, not decision authority. Its one permitted write records a non-PII interaction only.
